# OLTP vs OLAP

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Feature                 OLTP                    OLAP
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  **Full Form**           Online Transaction      Online Analytical
                          Processing              Processing

  **Purpose**             Handles day-to-day      Supports data analysis
                          business operations     and reporting

  **Data Type**           Mostly current and live Mostly historical data
                          data                    

  **Queries**             Simple and fast queries Complex queries
                                                  involving large amounts
                                                  of data

  **Operations**          Insert, Update, Delete  Mostly Read
                          (and Read)              

  **Users**               Customers and           Data analysts,
                          operational employees   managers, and business
                                                  teams

  **Speed**               Very fast transaction   Optimised for fast
                          processing              analysis of large
                                                  datasets

  **Examples**            ATM transactions, UPI   Sales dashboards and
                          payments, and shopping  business reports
                          apps                    

  **Storage**             Typically relational    Typically data
                          databases               warehouses
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
